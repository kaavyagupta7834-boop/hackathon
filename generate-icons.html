CodeLens Icon Generator

Preview, then download all three sizes in one click.

What to do next:
  1. Click "Download All Icons". Three PNG files land in your Downloads folder.
  2. Move icon-16.png, icon-48.png, and icon-128.png into this hackathon folder (next to manifest.json).
  3. Go to chrome://extensions and click the reload icon on CodeLens.
  4. The "N" placeholder is replaced with the proper CodeLens icon.
